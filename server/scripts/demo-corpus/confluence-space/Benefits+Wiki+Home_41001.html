1. Benefits Wiki

Benefits Wiki Home

This wiki is where the benefits team wrote things down between 2016 and 2024. It was never the plan document and was never approved by anyone; it is kept because the reasoning in it is not written down anywhere else.

What is in here

  • The enrolment FAQ, which answered the questions members actually asked at the desk
  • The claims desk notes, written by whoever was on the desk that week
  • A cheat sheet for coordination of benefits, which nobody ever managed to make short
  • An old plan summary, kept only so we can see what we used to tell people

What is not in here

Anything official. Where this wiki and a plan document differ, the plan document is right and this wiki is a record of what somebody believed at the time.
