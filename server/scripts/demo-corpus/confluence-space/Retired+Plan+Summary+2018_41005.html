Retired plan summary (2018)

Pasted out of the old intranet in 2019 and never tidied. Retired: do not quote this to a member.

It is kept so we can see what we told people in 2018, which comes up in appeals & in complaints, as does what we told them about AT&T and R&D coverage.

The 2018 plans were not tiered: if the allowed amount was < 500 the member paid nothing, and if it was > 500 they paid twenty per cent.

Deductible 2018: $900 individual, $1,800 family, and the out-of-pocket maximum was $4,500 individual.

  • Preventive care was covered in full, as it is now
  • Mental health had a twenty-visit annual limit, which was removed in 2020
  • The out-of-network rules were in a separate PDF that did not survive the migration
