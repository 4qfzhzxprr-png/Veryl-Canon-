Enrolment FAQ (2019)

Written for the 2019 open enrolment and patched every year afterwards without changing the title. Dates and figures in it are the 2019 ones unless a line says otherwise.

When does enrolment open?

1 November, closing at midnight on 30 November. Elections made outside that window need a qualifying life event, and the event has to be evidenced within thirty days of it happening.

What happens if I do nothing?

Last year's elections roll over, except the health savings account, which has to be re-elected every year. This surprises somebody every November.

When does coverage start?

Situation Coverage begins
Open enrolment election 1 January
New hire First of the month after the waiting period ends
Birth or adoption The date of the birth or placement, backdated
Marriage or partnership First of the month following the event

What the desk gets asked most

  1. Whether a stepchild counts as a dependant. Yes, on the same evidence as any other child.
  2. Whether a plan change can be undone after the window closes. No, and this is the one people are angriest about.
  3. Whether the deductible resets on the plan year or the calendar year. Plan year, and they are the same thing here.
