Dependant evidence checklist

What the desk asks for, per relationship. This page was restricted while the space was exported, so the index does not list it; its place in the tree comes from the breadcrumb trail above.

  • Spouse or partner — marriage or civil partnership certificate, or a joint financial document at least six months old
  • Child under 26 — birth certificate, adoption order, or the court order placing them
  • Stepchild — the child's birth certificate and the certificate for the marriage or partnership
  • Disabled adult child — the above, plus the medical certification, renewed every two years
  • Any other dependant — decided case by case, and the decision is written down whichever way it goes

What we do not keep

Take the evidence, record that it was seen and by whom, and give it back. The desk does not hold copies of birth certificates, and never has.
