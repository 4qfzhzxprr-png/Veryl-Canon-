Claims desk notes

Whoever holds the claims desk writes here. It is not a procedure and has never been reviewed; it is the list of things that took somebody an afternoon to work out.

Pended claims

  • Pend for one reason at a time. A claim pended for three things gets one reply that answers one of them.
  • Say what is missing in the letter, in the words the provider's billing system uses, not ours.
  • The fifteen-day clock starts when the information lands, not when somebody picks it up.

Manual pricing overrides

An override needs the reason typed into the claim, not into an email. Overrides are sampled monthly and an override with no reason on it is a finding against the desk, not against the examiner.

Reprocessing after a correction

When a policy correction goes back further than ninety days, the reprocessing run is scheduled rather than triggered by hand, because the last time somebody did it by hand it ran twice and two thousand members got two letters.

How long the desk keeps things

Everything on a claim stays in the claim system and nothing is kept on the shared drive. How long the claim system keeps it is somebody else's page; the desk's rule is that a claim file is never deleted by a person.

Last written to in March 2024, when the desk moved to the new queue tool.
