Formulary Change Log 2025

Every change the P&T Committee made to the formulary in 2025, with the date it took effect and the notice given. Kept by pharmacy outside the wiki because the committee wanted one file it could print for the minutes.

First quarter

  • Two biosimilars added at tier 2, effective 1 February, sixty days' notice to prescribers and none required to members because nothing moved up a tier.
  • One brand moved from tier 2 to tier 3 on the availability of a generic, effective 1 March, ninety days' notice to affected members with the generic named in the letter.

Second quarter

  • Step therapy removed for one class after the committee reviewed the evidence and found the preferred agent no longer first line. Effective immediately on decision, which the notice rules allow when a change is in the member's favour.
  • Quantity limit tightened on one analgesic, effective 1 June, with an exception route named on the same letter.

Third and fourth quarters

Four additions, no removals, and one specialty drug moved to the split-fill programme after two quarters of waste data. Effective dates 1 August and 1 November. The split-fill change is the one that generated the most calls; the desk script for it is in pharmacy/split-fill-script.

The notice rule the committee works to

A change that removes a drug or raises a member's cost gets ninety days' notice and names an alternative. A change in the member's favour takes effect at once. A change that affects nobody currently taking the drug gets sixty days' notice to prescribers only.
